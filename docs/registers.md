# Historical registers

Open Operations > Information register or Operations > Accumulation register.
Available registers display stored residents and registrations, responsible
persons, account services, additional properties, family relationships, tariffs,
individual and shared readings, meter status history and shared-meter users.
Accumulation views also show recorded meter differences, posted service charges
and posted service volumes.

Each register opens in its own workspace window and initially shows the complete
stored history. Refresh, Filter and Clear filter appear on the table toolbar.
Filter opens a separate window with field, comparison and value columns, selection
commands, OK and Cancel. Date comparisons use calendar dates independently of
the chosen display format. Text search and an attention filter are also available.
Click a column heading to sort. Tables display 100 records per page; exports and
printing include every matching record, not just the visible page.

Print, Save PDF, XLSX and CSV are separate commands. XLSX keeps account numbers
as text, applies suitable numeric precision and column widths, and freezes the
header. CSV does not store widths or colors. PDF export requires the Windows
desktop application.

Registers are read-only views. Opening or exporting one does not post documents,
change readings or recalculate closed months. A register may be empty when no
corresponding records exist or when the selected filter excludes them.

Monthly posted charges and volumes remain monthly records. A daily date range
includes a monthly record when the month overlaps that range; the register does
not invent daily posting dates or daily amounts. Consumption rows show differences
between consecutive stored readings; initial readings do not create consumption.
Negative differences are highlighted for review and are not automatically repaired.

Tariff type and excess-rate columns remain blank when these values are not
preserved in the portable database. Blank cells do not assert a zero rate.
