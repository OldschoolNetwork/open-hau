# Receipt options in 26.2 development

Seven design choices are available: Studio, Compact, Classic, Ribbon, Botanical,
Heritage and Custom. Ribbon uses geometric accents; Botanical adds restrained
decoration. Heritage keeps a familiar payment notice above the main bill while
adding clearer tables, logical sections, an organisation logo and outline icons.
Readability does not depend on colour.

Custom provides a visual appearance editor for font family, borders, pattern,
heading alignment and economical background-free printing. Combine these with
the colour, type size and SVG/PNG/JPEG/WebP logo controls. Save named configurations
to reuse the choices. This is appearance configuration within an established
layout, not an unrestricted drag-and-drop page designer.

Receipts support Russian, Romanian, both together, or English. English controls
the built-in labels and number formatting. Service names, title and footer have
editable English fields. Personal names, addresses and other entered data are
not automatically translated.

The receipt panel includes a due-date control for the current accounting month.
An explicit monthly date is printed in both the payment notice and the main bill.
Other months retain the general next-month due-day setting. Restore the general
deadline to remove a monthly override. Calendar dates are validated; months may
use different deadlines, including dates after the 28th where valid.

Individual meter rows include previous/current readings, dates, serial numbers
and consumption. Physical consumption remains separate from billed volume and
shared differences. Missing dates from older aggregate records are shown as absent.

Review print preview for paper size, margins and page count before printing.
Long bills or larger type may require more pages. HTML export includes the design;
PDF is produced through the system print dialog.
