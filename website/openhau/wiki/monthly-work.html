Monthly workflow

Select the period

Verify the selected month, opening balances, connected services and applicable tariffs. Complete earlier months first: balances require saved historical charges.

Enter readings and quantities

Use the readings workspace for previous/current meter values or explicit quantities. Zero is a value, not missing information. Apply the edited readings, then save.

Typing into the grid does not commit the edits. Saving asks for pending readings to be applied first. Finish the entry before switching periods or closing.

Missing readings or tariffs produce warnings. Do not interpret an incomplete calculation as zero debt.

Record payments and adjustments

Choose the account, amount, payment date and entry date. The payment date determines the accounting month; entry date records when it was entered. Add a reference to the supporting document.

An adjustment belongs to its selected period. A positive adjustment increases charges; a negative one reduces them. Record a reason and retain the supporting document separately. Adjustments do not replace tariff history.

Reconcile

Review charges, payments, balances and warnings. Closing balance equals opening balance + charges including adjustments − payments. A negative balance is an advance.

For the first October 2026 demonstration account, charges are 1047.44 MDL. An October payment of 10.00 MDL with no opening debt leaves 1037.44 MDL. The second sample intentionally lacks readings; complete them before closing.

CSV exports a monthly summary. Detailed turnover/balance reports are not available in 26.1.

Receipts and closing

Inspect receipts before printing. Incomplete calculations are marked as drafts. Close the month, then save. Closing preserves charge snapshots and is blocked when required data or historical months are missing.

To correct an earlier closed month, reopen later closed months in reverse order first. Export a full backup before corrections, verify the result and close periods sequentially again. Reopening imported legacy history may recalculate it under the current application's rules; retain the original migration archive.
