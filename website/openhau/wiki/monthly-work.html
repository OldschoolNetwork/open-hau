Monthly workflow and reports

Prepare the period

Select the billing period from the period-change command. Review the wizard before completing any recalculation or change to saved charges.

Readings and shared meters

Enter dated individual and shared meter readings. Keep previous and current readings distinct. An absent reading is missing information, not zero usage.

Connect individual meters and participating accounts to their shared meter. Differences are allocated equally across eligible personal accounts. Exclusions apply to the selected month; excluded consumers remain part of the subtraction from shared consumption but do not receive a difference charge.

Waste collection

Set the number of chargeable residents for each account in the selected month. The charge uses this monthly count and the applicable per-person tariff.

Payments

Open Batch payment entry to add or fill account rows and enter amounts. Enter moves to the next amount; Shift-Enter moves to the previous one. The comment and action buttons stay below the scrolling table.

Payment details show the allocation by service. Automatic allocation uses the service debt proportions. A manual balancing adjustment can be assigned to the configured building-maintenance service. Applied historical documents remain available for inspection without silently recalculating their imported allocation.

Reports

Choose a report, select the date range and configure filters, grouping and sort order. Generate it before printing or exporting. Account and service balance reports, payment reports and registers offer the relevant PDF and spreadsheet exports. Printing and saving a PDF are separate actions.

Finish the month

Review missing readings, tariffs, adjustments and allocations. Prepare receipts, save the database and make an independent backup. Closing a period preserves its calculated history; reopening or rolling back requires deliberate review.
